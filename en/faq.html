FAQ

Who are you?

A small volunteer team of friends from northern Gaza. We document daily life in short videos. We do not publish our members' names, to protect their safety and privacy.

Are you registered?

We are currently working on licensing the team.

Can I donate?

Ways to support are being arranged; we will announce them when ready. To talk about supporting us, email marsa.team1@gmail.com or write to us on any of our pages.

Do you belong to a political party or group?

No. We are a small civilian team aiming for documentation and accurate information.

Can I use your footage?

All rights reserved to the team; to use our material, please contact us.
