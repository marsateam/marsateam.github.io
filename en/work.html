What we do

Four areas, and we grow in them step by step.

Documenting

Short videos (about half a minute) from daily life in northern Gaza, subtitled in Arabic and English so the meaning reaches people outside Gaza.

Water awareness

Water is the first thing people here ask about. We film the road to it and the effort and time it demands.

Sourcing and fact-checking

We aim to check every claim before posting and cite the source in the caption where we have one. If a fact changes, we correct it in public.

Partnerships

We look for established organizations and partners who can guide us and work with us transparently. We do not promise what we cannot deliver.
